Quality & Safety

QA/QC Framework & HSE Protocol

Every deliverable undergoes rigorous multi-stage quality verification to ensure statutory compliance and mathematical accuracy.

Quality Assurance Pipeline

  1. 1
    Control Network Verification

    Independent re-observation of geodetic baselines and published coordinates before any production survey begins.

  2. 2
    Field Data Audit

    Redundant cross-checks on angular closures, baseline residuals, and traverse misclosure tolerances.

  3. 3
    Office Processing & Peer Review

    Senior surveyor review of all computed coordinates, contour models, and CAD drawings before client submission.

  4. 4
    Statutory Seal & Lodgement

    Final survey plans stamped with SURCON seal and lodged with relevant state survey authorities.

Health, Safety & Environment

  1. 1
    Pre-Mobilization Safety Briefing

    Mandatory toolbox talks, PPE inspection, and site-specific hazard assessments before every field deployment.

  2. 2
    PPE & Equipment Compliance

    Full personal protective equipment issued: hard hats, high-visibility vests, safety boots, and first-aid kits.

  3. 3
    Environmental Protocols

    Minimal-footprint field operations with proper waste management and terrain rehabilitation post-survey.

  4. 4
    Incident Reporting & Review

    Zero-tolerance near-miss reporting system with structured corrective action reviews after every project phase.
