About Me

Precision Built on
Professional Foundation

I am Bayode Emmanuel Ozovehe, a registered surveyor certified by the Surveyors Council of Nigeria (SURCON) with extensive field experience across some of Nigeria's most demanding infrastructure and oil & gas projects.

From coordinating topographic surveys for an aircraft MRO hangar in Ikeja to leading field data collection across 183km of road rehabilitation in Adamawa State, I bring technical rigour, CAD proficiency, and sound engineering judgment to every project.

Through Pathways Mapping Nigeria Limited, I also offer cutting-edge drone mapping and aerial data collection services — giving clients modern, efficient survey solutions backed by professional registration.

I am currently available for private consulting engagements across Lagos and nationwide.
