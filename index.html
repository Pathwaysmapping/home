Corporate Profile & Overview

Setting the Benchmark for Technical Rigor & Geospatial Integrity

Pathways Mapping Nigeria Limited is an accredited Nigerian geospatial engineering consultancy firm incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act. Since September 2021, we have built an enviable track record of precision, innovative engineering, and dependable execution across aviation infrastructure, commercial and residential land administration, agricultural mapping, and construction surveys.

Multidisciplinary Consortium Capacity: While our primary specialized practice centers on Geomatics Surveying, Drone Photogrammetry, Construction Setting-Out, and Civil 3D Design, we operate an established consortium combining licensed in-house Geomatics Surveyors with retained COREN-registered Civil/Structural Engineers (Retained Civil Engineering Practice) and TOPREC-registered Urban Planners (Retained Town Planning Consultant) on flexible, ongoing contracts to deliver turnkey statutory land titling, soil foundation design review, and master layout approvals when required.

Quality & Safety Mandate

QA/QC Framework & HSE Standards

Zero tolerance for mathematical error, observational drift, or unsafe field practices.

4-Tier QA/QC Quality Framework

  • 1
    Instrument Calibration & Loop Redundancy

    All GNSS and Total Stations are calibrated to ISO 17123 standards. Observations enforce closed traverses and redundant baseline ties.

  • 2
    Computational Closure & Geodetic Verification

    Independent closure error calculations and coordinate transformations (UTM / Minna Datum) prior to CAD ingestion.

  • 3
    Multi-Disciplinary Technical Audit

    Civil blueprints and layout subdivision schemes undergo rigorous cross-disciplinary review by our Principal Surveyor and retained partners.

  • 4
    Statutory Sealing & Permanent Cloud Archive

    Formal stamping with appropriate professional seals and dual archiving in permanent physical registers and secure encrypted cloud storage.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

  • A
    Mandatory PPE Compliance

    Strict enforcement of high-visibility vests, steel-toe boots, hard hats, eye protection, and specialized field gear across all work zones.

  • B
    Pre-Mobilization HIRA & Toolbox Talks

    Daily safety audits and hazard identification conducted prior to instrument setup, drone takeoff, or field stakeouts.

  • C
    Environmental Stewardship

    Responsible field operations, preservation of natural boundary vegetation, and zero hazardous footprint on client properties.

  • D
    Statutory Regulatory Compliance

    Full adherence to Nigerian National Environmental Standards (NESREA) and State Safety Commission mandates.
