Company profile

A registered indigenous geospatial firm

Pathways Mapping Nigeria Limited is a registered indigenous geospatial and surveying company incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. We provide professional surveying, GIS, mapping and geospatial consultancy services tailored to support infrastructure development, environmental management, land administration, engineering projects and spatial data solutions across Nigeria.

The company combines modern surveying techniques with advanced geospatial technologies to deliver accurate, reliable and timely services to government agencies, private organisations, construction firms and individuals — the same combination of precision and regulatory rigour demanded by cadastral, engineering and mining survey work.

Vision To become a leading geospatial and surveying consultancy firm delivering innovative, accurate and technology-driven spatial solutions across Nigeria and beyond.
Mission To provide reliable and professional surveying, GIS and mapping services through modern technology, skilled manpower and quality service delivery that supports sustainable development and infrastructure growth.

Quality assurance

Quality is a discipline, not a final inspection

Every stage of data capture and processing carries a defined check, an acceptance tolerance and a named point of responsibility — so errors are caught where they occur rather than discovered downstream in a rejected plan or a boundary dispute.

  • Control survey — independent baseline redundancy and least-squares residual review against SURCON practice tolerance.
  • Traverse — angular and linear misclosure checked before adjustment, closed within control-grade tolerance.
  • Boundary corners — duplicated by independent occupation or checked against a total station tie.
  • Computation — every area and bearing figure cross-checked by a second method before release.
  • Document control — versioned deliverables with a traceable review and sign-off record.
Health, safety & environment

Field work planned around real site conditions

Survey sites are frequently remote, vegetated and seasonally accessible, and community and security liaison is often a precondition for safe mobilisation. Our HSE plan is built around those realities rather than a generic urban survey workflow.

  • Hazard identification and control measures agreed before every mobilisation.
  • Personal protective equipment issued and enforced for all field personnel.
  • Emergency response and incident reporting procedures in place on every site.
  • Community liaison and host-community notification carried out ahead of field entry.
  • Environmental care — minimal clearance, waste removal and site reinstatement after survey.
