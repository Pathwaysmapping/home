How we work

Control before detail. Regulator-ready, first time.

No boundary, topographic or cadastral detail is captured until survey control has been independently established and tied to the national geodetic reference framework. That discipline is why our coordinates remain defensible in isolation from the crew that captured them.

Method fit for terrain. GNSS static and RTK, total station, and UAV photogrammetry are complementary, not interchangeable. We select the technique that suits the canopy, access and precision requirement of each part of a site rather than forcing one method across a variable area.
1

Desk study & reconnaissance

Title and coordinate review, cadastral cross-check, and a site visit for access, canopy, CORS coverage and community liaison requirements.

2

Control establishment

Static GNSS observation tied to CORS and the national network, adjusted as a least-squares network before any detail is captured.

3

Boundary & detail survey

RTK GNSS, total station and UAV photogrammetry selected by terrain, with redundant occupation of every boundary corner.

4

Office processing

Baseline and traverse computation, UAV aerial triangulation, DTM and orthophoto production, area and bearing computation.

5

Quality assurance

Independent check of every computation, plan and report against defined acceptance tolerances, with a named responsible person at each stage.

6

Certification & handover

Plan certified by the Principal Surveyor, lodgement package compiled, GIS dataset and all field records handed over to the client.
