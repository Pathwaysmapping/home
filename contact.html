After you send it

What happens next

STEP 01

We acknowledge

Every enquiry is acknowledged. WhatsApp gets the fastest response; email is answered within one working day.

STEP 02

We clarify

A short call or site visit if the scope needs pinning down — better to ask now than to quote for the wrong survey.

STEP 03

You receive a quotation

Written scope, methodology, accuracy statement, deliverables, programme and price, valid for a stated period.

STEP 04

We mobilise

On acceptance, we agree access, community liaison and dates, then mobilise with the right equipment for the terrain.
