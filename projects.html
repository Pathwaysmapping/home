What this record demonstrates

Transferable capability, not just a list of sites

The disciplines behind these projects are the same ones that underpin cadastral, mining and land administration work.

Control establishment at scale

Fixing and adjusting control networks across 183 km of corridor demands the same rigour as establishing control over a remote mineral concession — and the same tolerance for field logistics.

Precise coordinate determination

Aircraft hangar setting-out tolerances are unforgiving. That precision transfers directly to boundary corner coordination where a centimetre determines a title dispute.

Plans fit for lodgement

Producing drawings that engineers, contractors and clients accept without rework is the same skill as producing plans a registry or the Mining Cadastre Office accepts without query.

Multi-site programme delivery

Running three Abuja terminal sites concurrently proved the scheduling and reporting discipline needed for multi-title or multi-parcel assignments.

Design-grade CAD output

Virgin road grade design in Civil 3D to full standards compliance means CAD is a design capability here, not just a drafting service.

North-East to South-West coverage

Delivery in Lagos, Abuja, Adamawa, Bauchi and Ondo demonstrates genuine nationwide mobilisation, including in security- and access-constrained regions.
