Pathways GIS Institute

A geospatial training institute for Akure, Ondo State — drone mapping, GIS, remote sensing, RTK GNSS practice and AI for spatial analysis, taught on live project data by a registered surveyor.

The plan

Geospatial training Ondo State currently has to travel for

Nigeria's geospatial sector is growing faster than the supply of people trained to work in it. Drone mapping, GIS, remote sensing and spatial data science are now routine requirements on infrastructure, mining, agriculture and land administration projects — but practical, instrument-based training in these skills is concentrated in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.

Pathways GIS Institute is being established in Akure to close that gap. Our curriculum is built by a practising, SURCON-registered surveyor around the work we actually deliver — control networks, boundary surveys, UAV flights and cadastral plans — so participants leave able to do the job, not merely describe it.

Who these programmes are for

Five groups we are building this for

Students & graduates

Surveying, geography, geology, civil engineering and environmental science students who need instrument hours and software fluency their department cannot provide.

Practising surveyors

Professionals adding UAV, GIS or spatial data science to an established survey practice — and building a CPD record.

Pilots who can fly but cannot yet turn imagery into a survey-grade orthophoto, DTM or volume report.

Title officers, geologists and land administrators who need to read, check and produce cadastral spatial data.

Educators

Polytechnic and university lecturers refreshing practical skills on current software and instruments.

Delivery formats

Three ways to take a programme

Same curriculum and same certification. Choose the format that fits your location, schedule and budget.

Physical — Akure

Hands-on classroom and field training at our Akure facility. Supervised instrument sessions on RTK GNSS, total station and UAV, with field practicals run on live project sites around Ondo State.

  • Small cohorts for real instrument time
  • Field days on actual survey sites
  • Weekday and weekend streams

Live online

Instructor-led cohorts you can join from anywhere in Nigeria. Live sessions rather than pre-recorded video, with datasets supplied, exercises reviewed individually, and recordings available for revision.

Corporate on-site

  • Skills-gap assessment and report included

Certification & recognition

Building a certificate that carries weight

A training certificate is only worth what the market believes it is worth. Rather than issue paper and hope, we are building recognition deliberately — through the standard of the training itself, and through formal relationships with the organisations that employ our graduates and regulate the profession.

What every participant receives

  • A certificate of completion issued by Pathways Mapping Nigeria Limited (CAC Incorporated) and signed by a SURCON-regulated surveyor.
  • A verifiable certificate reference that an employer can confirm with us directly.
  • A documented syllabus and assessment record showing exactly what was covered and demonstrated.
  • A portfolio deliverable — a real plan, map, model or dataset produced during the course, to show at interview.
On accreditation, plainly stated. Certificates are currently issued on the authority of Pathways Mapping Nigeria Limited and its Principal Surveyor. We are actively pursuing institutional and regulatory partnerships to strengthen formal recognition, and we will state precisely which bodies are involved once those agreements are signed — not before.

Partner with the institute

We are actively seeking partners whose involvement makes this certificate more valuable to the people who earn it. If your organisation fits any of the categories below, we would like to talk.

  • Employers — construction, mining, energy, agriculture and consultancy firms willing to recognise the certificate in hiring, or to host placements.
  • Regulators & professional bodies — for CPD recognition and curriculum endorsement.
  • Tertiary institutions — universities and polytechnics seeking practical training partners for students and staff.
  • Government & MDAs — state ministries and agencies building geospatial capacity in Ondo State and beyond.

Our approach

Why train here rather than anywhere else

01

Taught by a working surveyor

Not a full-time trainer. Instruction comes from a SURCON-registered surveyor still delivering projects, so what you learn reflects current field and regulatory practice.

02

Real instruments, real hours

Cohort sizes are capped so every participant gets meaningful time on the RTK GNSS receiver, total station and UAV — not a demonstration from the back of a room.

03

Live project data

Exercises use anonymised data from actual survey jobs — messy, incomplete and realistic — rather than clean textbook datasets that never resemble the real thing.

04

Output you can show

Every course ends with a deliverable you keep: a certified-style plan, an orthophoto, a DTM, a dashboard or an analysis report to put in front of an employer.

05

Ondo State based

Serious practical geospatial training without the cost and disruption of relocating to Lagos or Abuja for weeks at a time.

06

A route to work

Strong participants are considered first for field assistant and CAD/GIS roles on our own projects, and referred to partner employers as those relationships are established.
