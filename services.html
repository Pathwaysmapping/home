01 — Engineering Survey

Survey control that civil works can be built against

Pre-construction control networks, dimensional control during build, and the measurement support that engineering design and claims depend on. We establish the reference framework first, so every subsequent measurement on your site relates to a single, verified datum.

  • Primary and secondary control network establishment and adjustment
  • Benchmark transfer and vertical control by precise levelling
  • Earthworks volume computation and cut/fill balance reporting
  • Deformation and settlement monitoring on structures and embankments
  • Technical reports and engineering recommendations to project management

Typical deliverables

  • Control point schedule with adjusted coordinates and accuracy statement
  • Site plan and control diagram in DWG and PDF
  • Volume computation report with method and sections
  • Certified technical survey report

02 — Topographic & Contour Survey

Know the ground before you design on it

Detailed elevation and terrain mapping for construction planning, drainage design, site feasibility and earthworks estimation. Captured by RTK GNSS, total station or UAV depending on the site's size, canopy and access — and delivered in the formats your design team actually works in.

  • Spot heights, break lines and contour generation at your specified interval
  • Digital terrain and surface model development
  • Existing features, services, structures and vegetation capture
  • Drainage catchment and flow direction analysis

Typical deliverables

  • Contoured topographic plan (DWG, PDF, printed)
  • Civil 3D surface / DTM file
  • Point cloud or coordinate listing (CSV)
  • GIS-ready layers (SHP / GeoPackage)

03 — Construction Setting-Out

Built where the drawing says it should be

Precise on-site layout of structures, roads, drainage and infrastructure so the build conforms exactly to the engineering design. Delivered on the aviation hangar, road and bus-terminal projects in our portfolio, and available for contractors on programme.

  • Building grid, column and foundation setting-out
  • Road centreline, offset pegs, levels and formation control
  • Drainage invert levels and pipeline alignment
  • Verticality, alignment and dimensional checks during construction
  • Resident surveyor engagement for the duration of a build

Engagement options

  • Call-off — scheduled visits at each construction stage
  • Resident — full-time site surveyor for the contract period
  • Verification — independent check of another contractor's setting-out

04 — Cadastral & Boundary Survey

Boundaries that hold up in court and at the registry

Accurate demarcation and documentation of land boundaries for individuals, developers, institutions and legal practitioners. Beacons placed at every corner and turning point, coordinated to a standard the relevant registry will accept, and certified by a registered surveyor whose name the regulator recognises.

  • Perimeter survey, beacon establishment and coordinate computation
  • Subdivision and layout design for estate development
  • Boundary re-establishment from existing survey records
  • Title verification support and technical reports for legal practitioners
  • Encroachment and boundary dispute investigation

Typical deliverables

  • Certified survey plan for registry lodgement
  • Beacon coordinate schedule with bearings, distances and area
  • Photographic beacon record
  • Technical survey report

05 — Mining Survey & Mining Cadastral

A mineral title is only as strong as the survey that defines it

Nigeria's solid minerals sector is in active transition. Mineral title administration has been digitised through the Mining Cadastre Office's eMC+ platform and enforcement of survey accuracy has tightened — coordinates lodged with the MCO must correspond to real, monumented, independently verifiable positions on the ground, free of the overlaps that lead to title disputes and rejected applications.

Title categories we survey

  • Reconnaissance permits and exploration licences
  • Mining leases and small-scale mining leases
  • Quarry leases and water-use permits

Scope of work

  • Boundary definition, re-establishment or verification against approved MCO coordinates
  • Beacon placement at all corner and turning points, with photographic records
  • Area computation aligned to the 20.25-hectare cadastral unit standard
  • UAV topographic support for pit design, haul road alignment and stockpile volumetrics
  • GIS-ready spatial database for exploration planning and investor due diligence
  • Lodgement package compiled for the MCO and, where applicable, OSGoF and the State Surveyor-General

Why our mining work is accepted first time

Compliance is designed in, not retrofitted.

  • Methodology built around the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007 and the 2011 Regulations.
  • Coordinates and plan format prepared against actual MCO and SURCON submission requirements throughout.
  • Control established from CORS-corrected static GNSS where no usable OSGoF beacon exists in range.
  • Field plans account for remote access, seasonal terrain and community liaison from the outset.

06 — Drone / UAV Mapping

Cover in a day what a ground crew covers in a week

UAV photogrammetry materially reduces field time on large, vegetated or difficult sites — and produces a permanent visual record of the site as it was on the day of flight. We fly, process and interpret in-house, with ground control tied to the same GNSS framework as our conventional survey work.

  • Photogrammetric flight planning and ground control point establishment
  • Orthomosaic and high-resolution aerial base map production
  • Point cloud, digital surface and digital terrain model generation
  • Stockpile and excavation volumetric measurement
  • Construction progress monitoring and periodic site documentation
  • Asset and infrastructure inspection imagery

Where UAV wins

  • Sites above roughly 5 hectares
  • Terrain that is unsafe or slow to walk
  • Repeat surveys for progress or volume tracking
  • Anywhere a visual record has evidential value

Dense canopy still requires ground methods — we combine UAV with total station traverse where the vegetation demands it.

09 — Route & Corridor Survey

Linear infrastructure, end to end

Long-corridor survey for roads, pipelines, transmission lines and canals — the discipline behind our 183 km Yola/Mubi and 25 km Itas/Gadau deliveries. Centreline definition, cross sections, strip topography and right-of-way mapping, produced directly into Civil 3D.

Engaging us

What happens after you make contact

No survey starts before both sides agree exactly what is being measured, to what accuracy, and what you receive at the end.

STEP 01

Brief

You send the location, the site extent and what the survey output is for. A call or site visit follows if the scope needs clarifying.

STEP 02

Written quotation

You receive a scope, methodology, accuracy statement, deliverables list, programme and price — not a bare figure on a phone call.

STEP 03

Mobilisation

On acceptance we agree access, community liaison and dates, then mobilise with the equipment the terrain requires.

STEP 04

Delivery

Certified plan, report and digital datasets handed over, with the field records retained and available should the work ever be questioned.
