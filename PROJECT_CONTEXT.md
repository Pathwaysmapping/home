## 3. Core Services Rendered
1. **Land & Cadastral Boundary Surveying**: Statutory perimeter plans, cadastral beacons, state ministry charting, GIS cadastral database integration.
2. **Topographical & 0.5m Contour Mapping**: Total station & RTK GNSS relief mapping, DTMs, elevation contours for flood analysis & site grading.
3. **Construction Setting-Out & Structural Control**: High-precision grid layout, pile centerlines, bolt alignment, tilt monitoring (e.g. aircraft hangars, high-rises).
4. **UAV Drone Photogrammetry & Orthomosaics**: High-resolution orthophoto generation, aerial DSM/DTM, volumetric stockpiles, multispectral agriculture.
5. **Civil 3D Infrastructure & Master Layout Subdivision**: Layout partitioning, road alignment corridor profiles, storm drainage networks, cut/fill balancing.
6. **GIS Spatial Databases & Oil/Gas Field Surveys**: Pipeline ROW corridors, oilfield routing, geospatial enterprise layers.

### Multi-Disciplinary Consortium Note:
- Pathways Mapping operates a consortium combining in-house Geomatics Surveyors with retained **COREN-registered Civil/Structural Engineers** and **TOPREC-registered Urban Planners** on ongoing, flexible contracts for statutory master layouts and foundation review.
- **CRITICAL**: Do **NOT** list *"Geotechnical Investigation & Soil Testing"* as a primary service rendered.
